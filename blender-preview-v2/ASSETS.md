# Asset provenance
- Original generated procedural geometry: Brachiosaurus, human, plants, terrain and props. Created for this project from its own V1 code; no third-party dinosaur model imported. Original geometry and procedural materials do not rely on purchased or scraped assets.
- HDRI: Kloppenheim 06 (Pure Sky), Greg Zaal / Jarod Guest, Poly Haven, CC0 1.0. https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky ; https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Download through official API, packed into Blender scene.
- Blender 4.5.3: official binary, https://download.blender.org/release/Blender4.5/ .
- Output is a silent five-second motion preview, not full episode. Native 1080x1920, 60fps, 300 frames, 128 maximum samples with adaptive sampling, CPU OIDN, motion blur. Visual quality and gait require review. V1 untouched.
